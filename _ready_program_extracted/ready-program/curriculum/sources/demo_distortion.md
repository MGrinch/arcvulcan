# Distortion control and balanced tack sequence (demo)

## Sequence before speed
- Thin panels can shrink and distort if heat is stacked into one area without a
  balanced sequence.
- Use a skip-weld or backstep sequence so shrinkage is spread across the joint
  instead of pulling one side first.

## Tack placement and restraint
- Tacks still matter for alignment, but distortion control is mainly about
  sequence, restraint, and heat distribution across the panel.
- A tack can lock stress into the joint, yet the broader fix is usually a more
  balanced welding sequence rather than repeated random tack removal.
