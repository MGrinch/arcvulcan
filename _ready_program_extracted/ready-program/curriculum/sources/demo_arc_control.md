# Arc length, angle, and undercut control (demo)

## When fit-up is already correct
- If the fit-up is sound but the weld still washes a groove at the toe, check
  arc length, travel speed, and work angle before blaming the joint.

## Common arc-control mistakes
- A long arc, excessive travel speed, or a poor angle can cause undercut at the
  toe even when the joint opening is consistent.
- Shorten the arc, keep a steadier angle, and pause long enough to wash the
  puddle into both sides of the joint.

## What this defect is not
- Do not assume every undercut signal comes from bad fit-up. Correct fit-up and
  bad arc control can produce overlapping symptoms.
