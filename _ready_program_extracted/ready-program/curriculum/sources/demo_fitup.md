# Fit-up, tacks, and defects (demo)

## Fit-up principles
- If the joint edges are uneven or the root opening varies, the weld bead will
  change size along the length and can create weak toes.
- Correct fit-up first: trim, grind, and re-align before adding more tacks.

## Tack weld guidance
- Use enough tacks to hold alignment, spaced consistently.
- If a tack pulls the joint out of position, remove it and correct alignment
  before welding.

## Common defect signals
- Uneven reinforcement, undercut at the toe, or lack of fusion often correlate
  with poor fit-up and inconsistent heat input.
